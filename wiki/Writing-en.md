# Writing

English · [Français](Redaction.md) · [Guide home](Home-en.md)

The **Writing** tab helps you write academic text with references **supported by
your documents**. It combines an editor with an **Assistant** panel.

## What the assistant offers

Each action has a **Run** button. It applies to the selected passage, or to the
whole document if nothing is selected.

| Action | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Find sources** | Find papers in your library supporting the passage |
| **Proofread** | Improve clarity, grammar and tone |
| **Suggest arguments** | Explore counterarguments, limitations and context |
| **Consult a notebook** | See what your notebooks say about the passage |

## Add citations

1. Write or paste your text into the editor.
2. Optionally select a passage to focus the search.
3. In **Find sources > Options**, you can guide the search by topic, authors or
   keywords, enable **Include sentences already cited** to find additional
   sources, and set **First citation no.** to continue existing numbering.
4. Click **Run**. Results arrive sentence by sentence, without waiting for the
   whole analysis to finish.
5. Place a suggestion in the text, then accept it. **Place all**, **Confirm ≥ 70%**
   and **Reject previews** process all suggestions at once.

### What to expect

- A citation is suggested only when a passage in your documents supports the sentence.
- A sentence that is too vague, or has no supporting passage, receives **no**
  citation. This is expected behaviour.
- Each suggestion shows the source, a supporting excerpt and a short explanation.

### Trace the original source

A suggestion from your library has an **Original source?** button. Bibliobrain
rereads the cited paper's passage and checks whether it credits the idea to an
earlier source. If so, it reports “This paper attributes this idea to an earlier
source”, along with the references and passage read.

- **Trace further back** repeats the check for one of those references if it is in
  your library. **Oldest credited source found** summarises the chain.
- **Identify** looks up a reference outside your library on Crossref: title,
  journal, year and publisher link. You must allow web access in Settings. Only
  the reference is sent, never your text.
- The result is not proof of originality. Attributions from a small model are
  indicative: check the passage.

## Proofread and develop arguments

- **Proofread** suggests corrections you can **Apply** or **Dismiss** individually.
- **Suggest arguments** proposes ideas grounded in your sources. Choose the
  **Argument type** in the options. Insert a suggestion as a sentence, paragraph
  or working note.

Responses follow the language of your text.

## Work on a selected passage

A toolbar appears above selected text: **Find citation**, **Rephrase**,
**Shorten** and **Copy for Word (Zotero)**. The last option copies the passage
with citations recognised by Zotero's Word plugin.

## History

**⋯ > History** keeps previous analyses and text versions, saved every 10 minutes
and before each bulk edit. **Restore** brings back an earlier version; the
replaced text remains in history.

## Bibliography and export

- **Copy**, in the editor's **⋯** menu, copies the text.
- **Export** creates a **Markdown**, **LaTeX** or **BibTeX** file.
- **Bibliography**, below the editor, lists all inserted references.
  **Copy full bibliography** copies them all at once.

Your draft is kept between sessions.
