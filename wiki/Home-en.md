# Bibliobrain

English · [Français](Home.md)

**A local, private academic research assistant.** Bibliobrain reads your PDFs (and
your Zotero metadata, if available), then lets you ask questions in plain language,
write with checked citations and discover new papers. With the default setup,
everything runs on your computer: your documents stay there.

> **Alpha version.** The interface and some behaviours change quickly. If something
> seems wrong, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting-en.md) or open an issue.

## Your first question in 5 minutes

1. Install Bibliobrain and keep the **Simple installation** option (see
   [Installation](Installation-en.md)).
2. On first launch, select your PDF folder (or let Bibliobrain detect Zotero), then
   let preparation finish (see [First launch](First-launch-en.md)).
3. When the indicator at the bottom of the sidebar says **Ready**, open **Chat** and
   ask a question, for example: “Which papers discuss [your topic]?”
4. Click a citation in the answer to check the passage in the paper.

The **Take the tutorial** button in **Settings > Overview** guides you through this
first question.

## The app in one minute

The left sidebar opens six areas:

- **Chat**: ask a question and get an answer grounded in your documents, with
  clickable citations and a panel showing the passages used.
- **Library**: browse your papers and their metadata, run one-click analyses
  (summary, methods, conclusions…) and copy references in APA or BibTeX format.
- **Discovery**: search online for papers through Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, arXiv,
  HAL and Crossref, and explore publication trends when you request it.
- **Maps**: explore citations within your library, a topic map and the sources
  behind the latest chat answer.
- **Notebooks**: create thematic subsets with their own notes and chat.
- **Writing**: use an editor that suggests sources supporting your text, sentence
  by sentence.

## Keep in mind

- Bibliobrain reads Zotero without modifying its database; it does not replace it.
- A reference is suggested only when a passage in your documents supports it.
- AI answers can be wrong. Always check the cited passage.
