# Notebooks

English · [Français](Carnets.md) · [Guide home](Home-en.md)

A **notebook** is a subset of your library focused on a topic: a thesis chapter,
literature review or course. Its chat and syntheses use only its sources, reducing
irrelevant results.

## Create a notebook

Click **+** beside **Notebooks** in the sidebar (**New notebook**), then choose:

- **Empty notebook**: give it a title, then add sources.
- **Zotero collection**: import papers from a collection. Later, **Update** adds
  new papers from that collection.

You can also create a notebook from chat (`/carnet`), from selected papers in the
Library (**Add to notebook**) or from a map (**Add**).

## Inside a notebook

The workspace has three columns:

| Column | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Sources** | **Add** free text or a PDF from the library, searching by title or author |
| **Notes** | Free-form notes, saved automatically |
| **Ask this notebook** | Chat limited to the notebook's sources |

The notebook's **⋯** menu offers **Citation graph** and **Clear chat**.

## Write a synthesis

**Write a synthesis** reads each notebook source (up to 40 papers), groups their
ideas into thematic chapters and checks citations against the papers' text. The
result appears in chat and is added to the notes. Allow a few seconds per paper;
many sources or a modest computer will take longer.

## Find related articles

**Find related articles** starts a [Discovery](Discovery-en.md) search based on the
notebook's sources and notes. Online search must be allowed.

## Notebook citation graph

**⋯ > Citation graph** shows how the notebook's papers cite one another, what they
cite and who cites them (see [Maps](Maps-en.md)).
